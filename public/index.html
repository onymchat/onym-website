Four parts.
No single owner.

Most messengers put identity, delivery, rules, and interface behind one company. Onym separates them, so one operator cannot take away the whole system.

01 · YOURS

Identity

Your identity is generated locally. Keys, contacts, group memberships, and message history stay on your device, never in an Onym account database. A twelve-word recovery phrase restores your identity on a new phone; Onym never receives it and cannot reset or revoke it.

No phone number. No email.
02 · OPEN

Messenger

The iPhone and Android clients are open source and MIT-licensed. End-to-end encryption happens on your device; chats and history remain local. Inspect the interface, cryptography, message formats, and network behavior before you trust it; then build or fork the app yourself.

A window, not a gatekeeper.
03 · REPLACEABLE

Courier

Nostr relays carry encrypted messages and invitations; Blossom servers store encrypted media. Operators see connection metadata—IP address, timing, and size—but never message plaintext or private keys. Choose another service, use several, or run your own.

The courier cannot read the letter.
04 · VERIFIABLE

Notary

Stellar contracts enforce group rules against cryptographic membership commitments—not a readable member list, group name, or conversation. The ledger still reveals group-level metadata such as the governance model, capacity tier, update timing, and sometimes a member count.

The notary never sees the roster.

Ownership underneath.
A messenger on top.

You should not need to understand infrastructure to have a private conversation. Create an identity, make a group, invite people, and talk.

  1. 01

    It starts as a messenger.

    Your chats, groups, and private conversations are where you expect them. No architecture lesson required.

  2. 02

    Open a chat and just talk.

    Messages and media feel familiar, while end-to-end encryption protects the conversation underneath.

  3. 03

    Then you discover the identity is yours.

    It lives on your device—not in an Onym account. Back it up, move it, or build the open-source client yourself.

  4. 04

    The courier is separate.

    Nostr relays carry encrypted envelopes. Use ours, choose another, or run your own—the courier never owns your identity.

  5. 05

    The notary is separate too.

    Stellar verifies group updates against hidden membership commitments. It never stores the roster, name, or conversation, though the governance model, capacity tier, and state-change timing are public. Identity, courier, and notary never collapse into one owner.
