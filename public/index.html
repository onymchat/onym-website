Four parts.
No single owner.

Most messengers put identity, delivery, rules, and interface behind one company. Onym separates them, so one operator cannot take away the whole system.

01 · YOURS

Identity

Your keys and history live on your device. There is no Onym account to suspend.

No phone number. No email.
02 · OPEN

Messenger

The iPhone and Android clients are open source. Inspect the code or build the app yourself.

A window, not a gatekeeper.
03 · REPLACEABLE

Courier

Nostr relays carry encrypted messages. Pick a relay you trust or operate your own.

The courier cannot read the letter.
04 · VERIFIABLE

Notary

Stellar contracts record group membership and rules. Use one you trust or deploy your own.

The notary does not carry the conversation.

Message
  1. 01

    Start with an identity you own.

    It is created on your phone—not registered with us. Back it up with your recovery phrase and take it with you.

  2. 02

    Create the place you want to talk.

    Name the group, write the invitation, and choose its rules. The group is not a row in our private database.

  3. 03

    Keep separate private lives.

    Each identity has its own contacts, groups, and history. Switch identity, and you switch inbox.

  4. 04

    Know what protects the group.

    Messages are end-to-end encrypted. No phone number is exposed. Group state is anchored separately from the conversation.

  5. 05

    Choose who carries your messages.

    Use the Onym relay, choose another Nostr relay, or run your own. The courier is replaceable—and never becomes your identity.

  6. 06

    Then just talk.

    The architecture stays out of the way. Underneath, each replaceable part keeps any single operator from owning the whole path.
